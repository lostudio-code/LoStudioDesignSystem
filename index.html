Elevation

Six levels, one shadow colour bound to Alias/Neutral/950. Deliberate deviation: on a #141414 canvas a black shadow is invisible, so dark mode substitutes a Border/secondary hairline plus a raised surface step. Shown on a light panel where the shadow reads.
